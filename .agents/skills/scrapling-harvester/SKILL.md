# Scrapling Harvester Guidelines

Based on [D4Vinci/Scrapling](https://github.com/D4Vinci/Scrapling).

## Incident Feed Harvesting
1. **Adaptive Selectors**: Resilient parsing of diverse cloud status pages and outage incident feeds.
2. **Keyless Ingestion**: Reliably collect real-world cloud events (GitHub, Open-Meteo, Statuspage) without credentials.
3. **Graceful Fallbacks**: Offline seed caching ensures zero-downtime demonstration even without an active internet connection.
