# UI/UX Pro Max Guidelines

Based on [nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill).

## Patterns
- **Bento Grid Architecture**: Compact, information-dense visual cards with variable proportions and consistent gutters.
- **HUD & Telemetry Badges**: Monospace status indicators, pulsing beacon dots, and live trend sparks.
- **Glassmorphic Cockpit**: Layered semi-transparent panels with backdrop blur, fine hairline borders, and glowing corner brackets.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts & Quick Actions**: Fast access controls for chaos experiments and telemetry resets.
